Purpose of This App

CottonGreen AI is specifically designed and trained to diagnose diseases in cotton plant leaves only. It uses an EfficientNetB3 model trained on 7 cotton leaf disease categories.

📸
Tips for Best Results
  • Upload a clear photo of a single cotton leaf
  • Make sure the leaf fills most of the image frame
  • Use good lighting — avoid shadows and glare
  • Avoid including other objects, hands, or backgrounds

About CottonGreen AI

About the Project

CottonGreen AI is a deep learning system designed to help farmers and agronomists detect cotton leaf diseases quickly and accurately using a smartphone or computer.

The model was trained on thousands of cotton leaf images across 7 categories, achieving over 95% test accuracy.
